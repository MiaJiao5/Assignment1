# Process

## Tools and Their Purposes
Multiple AI tools were used solely for translation and linguistic polishing in this essay. The original Chinese draft with all core arguments was independently conceived and written by me. Gemini and VS Code Chat AI assisted with English translation, which I carefully cross-checked against my original text. Doubao provided suggestions to refine the academic tone and sentence flow. I personally reviewed, selected and revised these AI recommendations. No AI generated the foundational content of this work; all final checks were completed by myself to maintain my original intent.

## What I Kept and Why
I leveraged AI to elevate my vocabulary to a more academic register. Where my original expressions were plain or colloquial, AI suggested professional terminology and well-structured phrasing appropriate for academic papers, improving the rigor and force of the arguments.

## What I Rejected and Why
I rejected AI-generated content marked by meaningless repetition, overexpansion, or fabrication. This filler material strayed from my core argument and resulted in redundant, generic prose. I cross-checked outputs from different AI tools against my own understanding to edit and rewrite these sections, ensuring every line supports my perspecives.

## Improvement and reflection
For future work, I plan to compose initial drafts directly in English to avoid possible semantic loss in Chinese–English translation.
